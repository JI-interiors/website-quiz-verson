LOCAL SERVICE AREA

Interior design for New Town.

New Town is a major planned urban area in the Kolkata metropolitan region. Linva Interiors serves customers in New Town who are planning interiors for apartments, homes, offices and other spaces.

We can coordinate complete interior projects or selected requirements such as modular kitchens, wardrobes, living rooms, bedrooms, furniture, electrical work, flooring, painting and finishing. Design and material recommendations are adapted to the space and budget.

Customers can use our budget calculator for an indicative planning estimate and then discuss the detailed scope with us. Final pricing depends on the approved design, measurements, materials and project requirements.

Nearby areas we can discuss as part of our wider service area include: Rajarhat, Salt Lake, Kolkata, Baguiati, Kestopur.
