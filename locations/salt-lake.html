LOCAL SERVICE AREA

Interior design for Salt Lake.

Salt Lake, also known as Bidhannagar, is an established urban area of Kolkata. Linva Interiors serves customers planning new interiors, renovations and selected spaces in homes, apartments and commercial properties.

We can coordinate modular kitchens, wardrobes, living rooms, bedrooms, furniture, electrical work, flooring, painting and other interior requirements within the agreed project scope. Design recommendations are made around the customer’s space, use and budget.

Our approach is to make material and procurement decisions easier to understand. Customers can discuss options with us and receive relevant bill and material information for purchases handled as part of the project.

Nearby areas we can discuss as part of our wider service area include: Bidhannagar, New Town, Rajarhat, Kestopur, Kolkata.
