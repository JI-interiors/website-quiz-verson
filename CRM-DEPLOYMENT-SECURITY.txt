LINVA INTERIORS CRM — DEPLOYMENT NOTES

The CRM lives under /crm/ and is intentionally excluded from public search indexing.

Implemented:
- robots.txt Disallow: /crm/
- CRM HTML meta robots: noindex, nofollow, noarchive, nosnippet
- googlebot noindex directives
- optional Apache X-Robots-Tag via crm/.htaccess
- CRM is not included in sitemap.xml
- Public website pages remain crawlable.

IMPORTANT:
This development CRM currently stores records in browser localStorage. robots/noindex controls discovery/indexing; it is NOT access control. Before collecting real customer data in production, deploy the CRM behind server-side authentication and a protected database/API.

Recommended production URL:
https://linvainteriors.in/crm/

Keep the CRM out of public navigation and do not add it to the sitemap.
