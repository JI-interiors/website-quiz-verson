Privacy Policy

Effective date: 22 August 2026   |   Last updated: 22 August 2026

Important: This Privacy Policy explains how Linva Interiors handles personal data collected through this website, particularly information submitted through our enquiry and Free Home Visit forms. It is designed with India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 and the Digital Personal Data Protection Rules, 2025 in mind, including requirements that apply as and when the relevant provisions come into force.

1. Who we are

For personal data collected through this website, Linva Interiors acts as the organisation responsible for deciding why and how that personal data is processed. If you have a privacy question, want to withdraw consent, or want to exercise an applicable data-protection right, contact us at support@linvainteriors.in or call +91 8961698488.

2. Information we collect

When you voluntarily submit a lead or enquiry, we may collect the information you choose to provide, such as:

  • Name
  • Phone number
  • Location or locality
  • Property type
  • Project requirements or message
  • Your consent to this Privacy Policy and the time at which the consent was submitted

We may also receive basic technical information that is automatically made available by your browser or hosting environment, where necessary for security, operation and troubleshooting. We do not intentionally request sensitive personal data through our lead forms.

3. Why we use your personal data

We use enquiry information for specific purposes connected with your request, including:

  • Contacting you about your interior-design enquiry or Free Home Visit request
  • Understanding your project requirements and preferred location
  • Providing estimates, consultations or relevant service information
  • Managing and following up on enquiries
  • Maintaining enquiry records for legitimate business and administrative purposes
  • Protecting the website, preventing misuse and complying with applicable law

We do not sell your personal information.

4. Consent

Our lead forms use an unchecked, mandatory consent checkbox. You must actively tick the checkbox before a form can be submitted. The form records the consent response and submission timestamp with the enquiry. Consent is requested in clear language and is not pre-selected.

You may withdraw consent by contacting us at support@linvainteriors.in. Withdrawal does not affect processing that was lawfully carried out before withdrawal, and it may not require us to erase information that we are legally required or otherwise permitted to retain.

5. Sharing and service providers

We may share personal data only where reasonably necessary to operate the website, receive and respond to enquiries, protect our systems, or comply with law. Our lead forms currently use a third-party form-processing service to transmit submitted enquiries to our business email. Such service providers process information for the relevant operational purpose and should maintain appropriate security and privacy safeguards.

We may also disclose information where required by law, lawful government or regulatory requests, court orders, or to establish, exercise or defend legal claims.

6. WhatsApp and external links

This website provides links to WhatsApp and other external services for communication or convenience. Once you leave the Linva Interiors website, the privacy practices of the external service apply. Please review the relevant service's privacy policy before sharing personal information there.

7. Cookies and analytics

At the time of this policy, the website does not intentionally use advertising cookies or third-party analytics scripts to build advertising profiles. Essential technical mechanisms may still be used by the website, hosting provider or third-party services to operate, secure or deliver requested functionality. If our use of cookies or analytics materially changes, this policy will be updated as appropriate.

8. Data retention

We retain enquiry information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this policy, including responding to enquiries, maintaining appropriate business records, resolving disputes, preventing fraud or misuse, and meeting legal or regulatory obligations. When personal data is no longer required and there is no lawful reason to retain it, we will take reasonable steps to delete or anonymise it.

9. Security

We take reasonable technical and organisational measures appropriate to the nature of the personal data we process to protect it against unauthorised access, misuse, alteration, disclosure, loss or destruction. No internet transmission or storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

10. Your rights and choices

Subject to the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, the Rules made under it, their applicable commencement dates, and other applicable law, you may have rights concerning your personal data, including requesting access to information about processing, correction or updating of inaccurate data, erasure where applicable, withdrawal of consent, and grievance redressal. Requests should be sent to support@linvainteriors.in.

We may need to verify your identity and may ask for information reasonably necessary to process a request. We will respond within the period required by applicable law.

11. Children's data

This website is intended for general audiences and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly solicit personal data from children through our lead forms. If you believe a child has provided personal data to us, please contact us so we can review the request and take appropriate action.

12. Changes to this Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when our website, services, data practices or applicable legal requirements change. The latest version will be published on this page with a revised “Last updated” date.

13. Contact and grievance redressal

For privacy questions, consent withdrawal, correction requests or grievances relating to personal data processed through this website:

  • Linva Interiors
  • Email: support@linvainteriors.in
  • Phone: +91 8961698488

We will review and respond to grievances in accordance with applicable law and the processes in force at the relevant time.

14. Legal framework

This policy is intended to support transparent handling of personal data in India and is informed by the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 and the Digital Personal Data Protection Rules, 2025, as notified by the Ministry of Electronics and Information Technology. Applicable provisions may commence in phases, so obligations and rights are applied according to the law and rules in force at the relevant time.

For the official government publications, see the Ministry of Electronics and Information Technology.
