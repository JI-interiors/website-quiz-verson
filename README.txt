LINVA INTERIORS — MOBILE-FIRST HOMEPAGE

This is a new mobile-first homepage design inspired by the information hierarchy and discovery flow of modern interior design idea sites, including the Livspace reference supplied by the client.

Files:
- index.html
- style.css
- script.js
- images/

The homepage uses local WebP images for faster loading and the Linva Interiors SVG logo.

Form:
The Free Home Visit form posts to FormSubmit at the email configured in index.html. The first FormSubmit submission may require email activation.

Next pages can be added later under folders such as:
- design-ideas/
- services/
- projects/
- locations/

Do not overwrite the existing live website until this new version is approved.
