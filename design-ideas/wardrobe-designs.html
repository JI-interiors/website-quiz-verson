PLANNING GUIDE

What to consider before you design.

01

Layout

Start with circulation, dimensions and the way the space will actually be used.

02

Storage

Plan storage around the things you use most so the finished space stays organised.

03

Lighting

Combine natural light with practical and decorative lighting where needed.

04

Materials

Choose finishes based on appearance, maintenance and everyday durability.

05

Colour

Use a consistent palette to create a cohesive home and highlight focal areas.

06

Budget

Decide priorities early so design choices remain aligned with your project scope.
